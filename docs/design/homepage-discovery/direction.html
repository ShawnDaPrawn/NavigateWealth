I

Why the Age of Discovery

The brand is already a ship under sail, and the AI adviser is already called Vasco. The direction doesn't invent a metaphor — it finishes the one that's there.

I

Instruments, not decoration

The ornament is functional-looking: wind roses, graduated limbs, rhumb lines, latitude rules. Every flourish reads as an instrument for finding your way — which is exactly the promise an independent adviser makes.

Compass rose · Astrolabe ring
II

Gilt earns its place

Gold appears as metal, never as a fill: hairline rules, corner ticks, numerals and a single gilt button per view. Scarcity is what makes it read as premium rather than gaudy.

One gilt action per screen
III

Chart paper, not white

Light sections move from clinical #FFFFFF to a warm parchment, watermarked with azulejo tile geometry. It flatters photography and makes the dark bands feel deliberate rather than default.

Parchment #F7F3EA
IV

Nothing moves

Navigation order, footer columns, section sequence and every word of copy stay exactly where they are. This is a re-skin with new type and new ornament — not an information-architecture change.

Same IA · Same content

Rules

One gilt button per viewport. Everything else is ghost or purple.
3px radius, never more. Sharp corners read as engraved; pills read as consumer app.
Hairlines carry gold, fills carry purple. Gold is never used as a large area of colour.
Alternating bands. Ink → parchment → dark grey → parchment → purple, so no two neighbours share a ground.

What does not change

Nav bar layout. 64px row, mark left, seven centred items in the same order, actions right.
Log In and Get Started stay. Same position, same order, same routes — /login and /signup.
Mega menus. Services, Solutions and Company keep their click-to-open panels and item copy.
Footer layout. Same five link columns, newsletter, socials and full compliance disclaimer.
Every section, in order. Hero → Services → Why Us → Partners → Reviews → Insights → FAQ → CTA.
All body copy. Only two additions: a serif line under each section eyebrow, shown in gold on these plates.

XIV

Taking It to Code

The direction is deliberately cheap to implement: it is mostly tokens, one font pair and six ornaments, with no change to routing, data or component structure.

Token changes

--primary#6D28D9 — unchanged
--gold#C7A24C — new
--gold-lt#E7CE96 — new
--section-white#FFFFFF → #F7F3EA
--section-dark-gray#313653 → #1C2027
--radius0.625rem → 3px

Most of the re-skin lands in src/styles/globals.css and src/index.css. Because --primary does not move, every component that already consumes it — buttons, badges, focus rings, the admin surfaces — inherits the new look without edits.

Sequencing

1 · Tokens and fonts. Add the gold ramp, parchment and Cormorant; self-host the variable font (~38KB latin subset).
2 · Ornament primitives. Six small components: WindRose, Graticule, CableRule, Cartouche, AstrolabeRing, Eyebrow.
3 · Nav and Footer. Shared shell first, so every other page inherits the direction immediately.
4 · Homepage sections. Top to bottom; each is a self-contained block in HomePage.tsx.
5 · Roll outward. Service pages, Solutions, Company, then the authenticated portal.

Open questions

Logo lockup. These plates set the wordmark in Cormorant beside the existing sail. The production PNG lockup drops in unchanged if you'd rather not touch the mark.
Contrast. Gold on ink measures 7.1:1 and passes AA at every size used here. Gold on parchment is reserved for rules and never carries text.
Serif headings. Section eyebrows here add a short serif line ("Charted for Every Passage"). Drop them and the direction still holds.
Photography. Current library works as-is under the vignette. A future shoot with warmer grading would lift it further.
“Navegar é preciso.”
Sailing is necessary. The old Portuguese motto of the navigators — and a fair description of what an independent adviser does for a living.
